Carbon Rim Impact Testing

Boyd Cycling & Time Bicycles Internship

Designed, fabricated, and iterated a custom "Guillotine" wheel-impact rig in a 72-hour sprint for UCI-aligned carbon rim durability testing.

Overview

Custom impact apparatus for validating carbon rim durability under controlled drop energy.

Challenge

Boyd needed a standardized impact tester immediately. No existing rig in the shop; three days to design and build something that could meet UCI-aligned drop testing.

Solution

Built a Guillotine-style tester with adjustable drop height and anvil mass. Frame from repurposed inventory shelving; guide rails and anvil from salvaged scrap, machined and welded on site. Electric winch with garage-door remote for safe, wireless raises. Supports multiple rim profiles without waiting on outside suppliers.

The Guillotine Rig

Controlled energy transfer to the rim structure

Rig in action - weighted anvil drop onto a carbon rim.

Function

Weighted anvil drops from adjustable heights to deliver controlled energy, simulating pothole strikes and similar road hazards. Used for UCI-standard testing and beyond.

Build Approach

Compressed timeline forced scavenged materials and in-house fab: shelving for structure, scrap for rails and anvil, winch for repeatable lifts. Result was a shop-ready precision instrument without lead times.
