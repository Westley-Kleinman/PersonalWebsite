Lantern Kart Rally

My one-day Mario Kart-style experiment grew into a local multiplayer kart racer. The laptop hosts the race while phones can act as controllers; solo play with computer racers works too.

The current local build has original karts, multiple tracks, items, drifting, and a Grand Prix mode.
